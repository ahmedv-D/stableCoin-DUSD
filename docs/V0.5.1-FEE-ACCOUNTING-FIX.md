**Status:** engine fixed + full suite / fuzz / crash / Monte-Carlo green.
**Scope:** swap-fee booking ONLY (70/10/15/5 native split). No other economic

---

## 1. Old accounting (the bug)

`core/dusd_v051_state.py` booked the gross swap fee as:

- POL depth: `+ fee * 0.80` (physically embeds 70% depth the **taller** 10% growth → 0.80).
- Insurance pool: `+ fee * 0.05` (real pool).
- Then **on top**, three provenance tallies: growth `+ fee * 0.10`,
  backers `+ fee * 0.15`, insurance `+ fee * 0.05`.

Resulting bookkeeping:

| bucket | atoms added | conservation-visible |
|---|---|---|
| POL | 0.80 | 0.80 |
| insurance pool | 0.05 | 0.05 |
| growth tally | 0.10 | **invisible** |
| backers tally | 0.15 | **invisible** |
| insurance tally | 0.05 | double-booked (0.05 tally on top of the 0.05 pool) |
| **total** | **1.15 × fee** | 0.85 visible / 0.30 invisible |

Failures of the old model:

1. **Under-count for conservation:** conservation-visible total was `0.95`
   (`0.80 POL + 0.05 insurance`) but attribution tallied `0.30` — i.e. the fee
   mass did not equal the sum of its visible parts. Adversarial suite flags the
   `1.15 + tallies` split as a fee double-count.
2. **Double-book via attributions:** growth/backer/insurance **tallies** added
   atoms on top of the physical pools instead of attributing atoms already embedded.
3. **Insurance counted twice:** `0.05` insurance pool **and** `0.05` insurance
   tally → 10% of fee accounted where only 5% is physically held.
4. Sum of allocations ≠ 100% of gross fee at conservation level (0.95 ≠ 1.00).

This is exactly the divergence row-7 of the V0.5.1 compliance audit
(`docs/V0.5.1-COMPLIANCE-AUDIT.md`). Fuzz/crash/MC only asserted generic
conservation bounds, so they never caught the split — the adversarial suite
owned the exactness.

## 2. Corrected accounting (canonical 70/10/15/5, all into backing)

`core/dusd_v051_state.py` now books the gross fee as:

- POL: `+ fee * 0.95` — physically embeds **70% depth + 10% growth +
  15% backers**.
- Insurance pool: `+ fee * 0.05` — the only separately-held pool share.
- The three tallies (growth `0.10`, backers `0.15`, insurance `0.05`)
  remain **pure provenance labels** that attribute POL/insurance depth atoms;
  they add **zero extra atoms**.

| bucket | atoms added | conservation-visible |
|---|---|---|
| POL | 0.95 (depth 0.70 + growth 0.10 + backers 0.15) | 0.95 |
| insurance pool | 0.05 | 0.05 |
| growth/backer/insurance tallies | 0.00 (attribution embedded in the atoms above) | 0.00 |
| **total** | **1.00 × fee** | **1.00** |

Properties satisfied:

- Sum of allocations = exactly **100%** of gross fee at conservation level
  (`0.95 + 0.05 = 1.00`).
- Nothing double-booked, nothing invisible, nothing double-counted.
- Conservation gain on both swap directions = **exactly the gross fee**.

## 4. Conservation check

`tests/test_adversarial_v051.py::test_I_fee_split_conserves_fee_exactly`
(line 158) asserts, float-only (no `ApproxScalar` arithmetic):

```python
pol_gain = s.pol.dero - d0_pol
ins_gain = s.insurance.dero - d0_ins
# CANONICAL conservation identity: 95% stays inside POL (embedding the growth
# and backer shares), 5% goes to the insurance pool.
assert (pol_gain - (d - fee)) + ins_gain == pytest.approx(fee, abs=1e-9)
assert (s.pol.dero - d0_pol) - (d - fee) == pytest.approx(fee * 0.95)
assert (s.insurance.dero - d0_ins) == pytest.approx(fee * 0.05)
# provenance tallies stay exact attribution labels:
assert (s.fee_pol_growth_dero - d0_growth) == pytest.approx(fee * 0.10)
assert (s.fee_backer_pool_dero - d0_back)  == pytest.approx(fee * 0.15)
assert (s.fee_insurance_dero  - d0_ins)    == pytest.approx(fee * 0.05)
```

Conservation is measured on native (DERO/DUSD) units directly off the same
pools that back the peg — no tracker-only fee amount represents economic asset
mass outside backing.
